RESOLVEGRAPH is an evidence-first commerce resolution infrastructure connecting buyers, merchants, and PayPal payment records in an auditable case workspace. Powered by the Bilateral Evidence-to-Remedy Reasoning (BERR) methodology and an interactive Knowledge Graph, ResolveGraph reconciles multimodal evidence (invoices, photos, tracking logs) to identify agreed facts, contested facts, and missing proof. A human-governed multi-agent copilot prepares policy-aligned remedies (refunds, replacements) that are executed seamlessly via PayPal Sandbox APIs upon merchant authorization.
