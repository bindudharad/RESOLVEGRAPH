# Problem & Solution Statement

## The Problem: The Fragmented Reality of Commerce Disputes
Modern online dispute resolution is fundamentally broken across three dimensions:

1. **Information Asymmetry & Fragmentation**:
   When a dispute arises, critical evidence is scattered across disparate systems. The merchant's warehouse logs live in an ERP, the buyer's unboxing photos are trapped in an email client, and the carrier's GPS proof of delivery is tucked inside a courier portal. Neither party sees a unified, objective record.

2. **Unstructured, Adversarial Arguments**:
   Traditional dispute portals invite freeform text arguments. This leads to emotional, conflicting claims, repeated requests for previously submitted information, and prolonged cycle times averaging 8.4 days per dispute.

3. **Disjointed Payment Settlement**:
   Dispute management software is disconnected from payment rails. Once a dispute manager agrees to issue a refund, the decision must be manually keyed into a separate gateway portal. This disconnect creates delays, clerical errors, and unnecessary bank chargebacks.

---

## The Solution: RESOLVEGRAPH
ResolveGraph transforms commercial dispute handling from an adversarial argument into a **collaborative, evidence-first reconciliation workflow**:

```
[Fragmented Email Threads]            [RESOLVEGRAPH]
[Unparsed PDF Attachments]   ──►   [Bilateral Knowledge Graph]   ──►   [Grounded Policy Remedy]
[Manual Gateway Refunds]           [BERR Reasoning Engine]             [PayPal Sandbox Settlement]
```

### Key Solution Capabilities:
- **Continuous Lifecycle Dossier**: Every case maintains an unbroken audit link connecting the original PayPal order session, capture transaction, customer claims, evidence files, and resolution outcomes.
- **Multimodal Evidence Normalization**: Automatically extracts and standardizes facts from binary documents (OCR parsing of invoices and computer vision inspection of drive labels).
- **Mutual Ground Truth**: The Bilateral Evidence Graph visually categorizes facts into **Agreed Facts**, **Contested Facts**, and **Missing Evidence**, eliminating misunderstandings before escalation.
- **Human-Governed Execution**: Artificial intelligence acts as an investigatory copilot to analyze evidence and prepare policy-compliant remedies, while human merchant managers maintain sole authority to disburse funds via PayPal Sandbox rails.
