## 2. Who Benefits from ResolveGraph?
- **For Online Merchants**: Reduces manual dispute investigation hours, mitigates costly chargeback penalties, enforces consistent return policy rules, and prevents return fraud via multimodal serial number verification.
- **For Consumers**: Eliminates repetitive requests for information, provides a transparent view of the evidence being evaluated, and delivers fast, fair partial refunds or replacements.
- **For Payment Networks (PayPal)**: Enhances dispute ecosystem data quality, reduces customer support escalation overhead, and provides auditable, structured evidence dossiers when formal arbitration is required.
