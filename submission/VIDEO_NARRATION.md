## Word-for-Word Spoken Script with Timecodes

> *This is **ResolveGraph**: evidence-first commerce resolution infrastructure connecting complaints, evidence, and PayPal settlement in one continuous workflow."*

---

### [0:15 - 0:35] Act 2: Purchase & Complaint Filing (42 Words)
> *"Let’s look at a realistic dispute. Alex purchases an ApexDrive one-terabyte SSD for seventy-nine dollars and ninety-nine cents, captured through PayPal Sandbox.*  
> *When the package arrives, the drive inside has only 512 gigabytes. Alex opens a dispute. In ResolveGraph, this initiates a structured, collaborative investigation."*

---

### [0:35 - 0:55] Act 3: Evidence Locker & Validation (38 Words)
> *"Both sides submit their documentation into an evidence locker: the merchant's invoice and the buyer's drive photograph.*  
> *The system validates binary magic bytes to block disguised executables and locks cryptographic SHA-256 checksums to ensure proof cannot be altered or tampered with."*

---

### [0:55 - 1:20] Act 4: Multimodal Extraction & Evidence Graph (52 Words)
> *"Multimodal AI reads the invoice table and parses the serial label on the physical SSD, normalizing one-thousand gigabytes and five-hundred-and-twelve gigabytes into standardized units.*  
> *The Bilateral Evidence Graph visually links every claim to its underlying document, providing transparent, symmetrical provenance for both the buyer and the merchant."*

---

### [1:20 - 1:45] Act 5: BERR Reasoning Engine Findings (51 Words)
> *"Our Bilateral Evidence-to-Remedy Reasoning engine evaluates numerical contradictions, semantic drift, and timeline order.*  
> *It mathematically calculates a discrepancy score of 0.88 and separates the facts: order and payment are agreed; storage capacity is contested; and outbound warehouse packing logs are missing."*

---

### [1:45 - 2:05] Act 6: Multi-Agent Copilot & Human Approval Gate (43 Words)
> *"Our multi-agent copilot assists with triage, policy evaluation, and remedy drafting.*  
> *Crucially, the AI is not an autonomous adjudicator. High-risk financial operations generate a cryptographically hashed approval request that requires explicit human merchant sign-off before execution."*

---

### [2:05 - 2:25] Act 7: PayPal Sandbox Settlement & Immutable Ledger (44 Words)
> *"With human authorization, ResolveGraph invokes the PayPal Sandbox Refunds API with an idempotent request key.*  
> *The twenty-five dollar refund is disbursed, the dispute transitions to Resolved, and the entire audit trail is permanently recorded in the immutable case ledger."*

---

### [2:25 - 2:45] Act 8: Research Rigor, Impact & Vision (37 Words)
> *"In benchmark evaluations across 300 cases, ResolveGraph achieved 89.3% accuracy while maintaining strict bilateral fairness.*  
> *By connecting complaints, evidence, and PayPal payments in one human-governed system, ResolveGraph brings clarity, speed, and fairness to global commerce. Thank you."*
