# RESOLVEGRAPH User Research — Merchant Interview Guide

## Study Title
Operational Efficiency, Fraud Reduction, and Policy Compliance in AI-Assisted Commerce Resolution.

## Participant Profile
- **Target Audience**: E-commerce merchants, store operators, dispute managers, or chargeback specialists handling >50 customer disputes or chargebacks per month.
- **Session Duration**: 50 minutes.
- **Format**: Semi-structured 1-on-1 interview + interactive workflow evaluation of RESOLVEGRAPH Merchant Dashboard.

---

## 1. Introduction & Context (5 minutes)
- Welcoming remarks and introduction.
- Contextual framing: "We are researching operational bottlenecks in e-commerce disputes, chargeback prevention, and how structured evidence reconciliation can protect merchant margins while treating legitimate buyers fairly."
- Confidentiality agreement and permission to record.

---

## 2. Current Dispute & Chargeback Operations (15 minutes)
1. **Volume & Cost**: "Roughly how many disputes or chargebacks does your store encounter monthly? What is your typical win rate on PayPal disputes or bank chargebacks?"
2. **Operational Overhead**: "How much time does your support team spend gathering order records, shipping slips, and chat logs to contest a dispute?"
3. **Friendly Fraud vs True Fraud**: "How do you distinguish between honest customer misunderstandings (e.g. carrier delay) and intentional friendly fraud (e.g. claiming an item was empty or damaged when it was not)?"
4. **Policy Enforcement vs Goodwill**: "How does your team decide whether to issue a full refund, partial refund, or reject a return that falls slightly outside your 30-day window?"
5. **Pain Points with Existing Tools**: "What frustrates you most about existing dispute portals (e.g., standard PayPal Resolution Center, Shopify disputes, or third-party chargeback representment software)?"

---

## 3. Walkthrough: RESOLVEGRAPH Merchant Command Center (20 minutes)
*Provide participant with interactive staging access to the RESOLVEGRAPH Merchant Operations Dashboard.*

### Task A: Automated Intake & Bilateral Discrepancy Analysis
- *Prompt*: "Inspect Case RC-0042 ('Defective Wireless Headphones'). Look at the BERR Discrepancy Breakdown."
- *Questions*:
  - "How helpful is it to see claims cross-referenced directly against warehouse serial records and carrier timestamps?"
  - "Does the discrepancy score (exact, semantic, temporal) give you immediate confidence on whether to contest or settle?"
  - "What do you think of the 'Missing Evidence' alert notifying you that a proof of delivery signature is still required?"

### Task B: Policy Grounding & Remedy Recommendation
- *Prompt*: "Review the remedy proposal generated by the PolicyRemedy specialist."
- *Questions*:
  - "The system checked your 30-day return window and 15% restocking fee rules automatically. Does this match how your best human agents reason?"
  - "Do you appreciate having candidate options (Full Refund, Partial Refund, Replacement, Store Credit) ranked by policy eligibility?"

### Task C: Human-in-the-Loop Financial Guardrails
- *Prompt*: "The AI has drafted an approval request for a $45.00 partial refund. Perform the approval action."
- *Questions*:
  - "How important is it to your CFO/finance team that the AI *cannot* disburse money autonomously without explicit operator sign-off?"
  - "Does the cryptographic argument hash preview give you confidence against tampering or unauthorized refunds?"

---

## 4. Debrief & Quantitative Rating (10 minutes)
Rate each statement from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree):
1. *"This interface would reduce our dispute investigation time by >50%."*
2. *"The bilateral evidence graph provides sufficient justification to win representments."*
3. *"The human-in-the-loop approval workflow satisfies our internal financial risk controls."*
4. *"The semantic drift tracker would help us catch packaging or supplier fulfillment errors early."*

**Final Question**:
- "If you could integrate this platform with your existing stack tomorrow, what would be the single most valuable integration (Shopify, PayPal, ERP, Zendesk)?"
